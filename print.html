How to print

  1. Print at actual size (100% scale — turn off “fit to page”). Each card slot is exactly 3.5 × 2.5 inches, landscape.
  2. Cut-out sheets hold 8 cards per page (2 columns × 4 rows). Dashed lines are your cut guides.
  3. Card backs are included — a backs page follows each card sheet. Print double-sided if your printer supports it, or print the backs separately.
  4. Cut out each card along the dashed lines (a paper trimmer makes this quick).
  5. Sleeve them. Cards are standard trading-card size turned sideways, so standard card sleeves fit. Slip a common playing card behind each print for extra stiffness.

You’ll need

  • A printer and US Letter paper or cardstock
  • Scissors or a paper trimmer
  • Standard card sleeves (optional, recommended)
  • A pile of 20–40 Treasure tokens — print extra card-size Treasures, or use coins or beads
  • A way to track life (starts at 20): scoresheet, counters or a phone
