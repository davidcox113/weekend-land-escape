The Cards

64 Wave 1 catalog cards across ten units, plus 35 Race Wave preview cards and 38 Wave 2 preview cards. Filter by unit, or tap any card to view the full art.

The Scrags

Fast, Forge-leaning goblinoids. Cheap aggression and tribal leadership — Raiders swarm early, Warlords lead the pack.

Ironfang Wave 1

An orc warband built on the shared keyword Aggressive (can attack the turn it enters). Big leaders reward you for holding a Fortification.

Dragon Wave 1 Draft

Legendary dragon mythics at the very top of the cost ladder — multi-mark mana plus heavy gold. Alternate versions and support pieces are included. Alternates are either/or choices for the same slot.

Fortifications

Structures use DEFENSE instead of power/toughness. They shield your troops and your economic expansions — walls reduce pressure, towers shoot attackers, gates soak hits meant for markets.

Economy Expansions

Gold comes IN each turn from buildings. Richer buildings have UPKEEP. No per-soldier wages.

Equipment Ladder

Pay to play, then pay again to Equip onto a creature. Big pieces can have upkeep. Equipment is your main gold OUTFLOW — without taxing every soldier.

Basic Lands

One land for each mana mark. Tap for one mana of that mark.

Hybrid Lands

Ally duals plus Crossroads Quarry. Tap for either listed mark (Crossroads Quarry: any color or Quarry, enters tapped).

Mana-Producing Artifacts

Mana rocks for color fixing and acceleration.

Treasure Tokens

Each Treasure = +1 Gold. Spend it, then return it to the shared pile. The card-size token is the Wave 1 standard (it matches the cards and shares sleeves); square chips and round coins are deluxe options.

Wave 2 Preview Preview

Introducing the Garrison mechanic: Fortifications can now house troops of a matching type, which protects them and powers up Sally attacks.

In development. These preview cards are drafts for playtesting. Names, costs and text may change, and they are not yet in the print-and-play PDF.

Equipment · 8 cards

Eight pieces of war gear from common pikes to a legendary siege hammer. Several reward a garrisoned bearer or a matching fort; mounts make any rider Mounted for Stables; siege gear finally gives non-dragon decks answers to big walls. Equip is paid in Gold unless a card says otherwise.

Fortifications · 14 cards

Fourteen structures built around the new Garrison keyword: barracks for Infantry, stables for Mounted troops, docks for Seafarers, an Ogre pen, a Dwarven hall, an Elven canopy, a dragon aerie, plus mid-to-high tier walls, towers, keeps, and one mythic citadel.

Economy · 8 cards

Eight Economy Fortifications. Five are Upgrades stacked onto existing Wave 1 buildings (lose both if destroyed); three are new: a catch-up trade route, a tax office that skims hoarders, and a guild hall that eases Supply. Net income stays at +1 to +3 per building.

Lands · 8 cards

Eight nonbasic lands: new hybrids covering missing pairs (Bastion/Quarry, Wildwood/Harbor, Forge/Quarry, Mire/Quarry), two tri-lands for the Dwarves and Humans, a fort-ramp land, and an instant-speed Garrison gatehouse. Every land with upside enters tapped or has a condition.
