The Cards

64 Wave 1 catalog cards across ten units, plus 35 Race Wave preview cards. Filter by unit, or tap any card to view the full art.

The Scrags

Fast, Forge-leaning goblinoids. Cheap aggression and tribal leadership — Raiders swarm early, Warlords lead the pack.

Ironfang Wave 1

An orc warband built on the shared keyword Aggressive (can attack the turn it enters). Big leaders reward you for holding a Fortification.

Dragon Wave 1 Draft

Legendary dragon mythics at the very top of the cost ladder — multi-mark mana plus heavy gold. Alternate versions and support pieces are included. Alternates are either/or choices for the same slot.

Fortifications

Structures use DEFENSE instead of power/toughness. They shield your troops and your economic expansions — walls reduce pressure, towers shoot attackers, gates soak hits meant for markets.

Economy Expansions

Gold comes IN each turn from buildings. Richer buildings have UPKEEP. No per-soldier wages.

Equipment Ladder

Pay to play, then pay again to Equip onto a creature. Big pieces can have upkeep. Equipment is your main gold OUTFLOW — without taxing every soldier.

Basic Lands

One land for each mana mark. Tap for one mana of that mark.

Hybrid Lands

Ally duals plus Crossroads Quarry. Tap for either listed mark (Crossroads Quarry: any color or Quarry, enters tapped).

Mana-Producing Artifacts

Mana rocks for color fixing and acceleration.

Treasure Tokens

Each Treasure = +1 Gold. Spend it, then return it to the shared pile. The card-size token is the Wave 1 standard (it matches the cards and shares sleeves); square chips and round coins are deluxe options.
