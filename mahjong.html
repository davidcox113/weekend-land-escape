Verdant & Ember Kingdoms · Mahjong Cards

Two mahjong stories share this page: Verdant / Bams, where patient care grows a kingdom, and Ember / Craks, where courage turns a private spark into a flame shared with all.

Mahjong cards only. These are American-mahjong-style Sudoku/story tiles (Verdant / Bams and Ember / Craks), hosted here for viewing only. They are not Weekend Land Escape game cards and are not used in that game.

Mahjong lesson: a Bam run is built one tile at a time; rushing breaks a good hand. Life lesson: small things cared for patiently grow roots that outlast storms.

Verdant Kingdom tiles

Mahjong lesson: a Crak run is built by answering what is offered, not by hoarding alone. Life lesson: a private spark stays cold, but a shared flame can warm a whole table.
