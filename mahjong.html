Three Kingdoms & Special Tiles · Mahjong Cards

Three mahjong kingdoms share this page: Verdant / Bams, Ember / Craks, and Tidal / Dots. Their stories are joined by Flowers, Winds, Jokers, and the Starborn Changeling.

Mahjong cards only. These are American-mahjong-style story tiles (three kingdoms, Flowers, Winds, Jokers, and Changeling), hosted here for viewing only. They are not Weekend Land Escape game cards and are not used in that game.

Mahjong lesson: a Bam run is built one tile at a time; rushing breaks a good hand. Life lesson: small things cared for patiently grow roots that outlast storms.

Verdant Kingdom tiles

Mahjong lesson: a Crak run is built by answering what is offered, not by hoarding alone. Life lesson: a private spark stays cold, but a shared flame can warm a whole table.

Ember Kingdom tiles · Craks / courage

Tidal Kingdom · The Ripple That Listened. Dots are watching: be still, listen closely, and let the message travel.

Tidal Kingdom tiles · Dots / watching

Flowers · The Fairies' Diary. The fairies watch the three kingdom stories and stitch them into one diary. Cards 1–4 echo the Threes peace signal; 5–8 are the fairies' own work as peacemakers. Lesson: someone has to notice, write it down, and keep inviting peace.

Flowers · The Fairies' Diary

Winds · The Messengers. Travelers who carry news between kingdoms. Lesson: news only matters if it arrives. East plants, South tends courage, West delivers, North listens. Same virtues as the kingdoms, carried on the wind.

Winds · The Messengers

Jokers · Wild. A joker fills a gap, but only if you know what your hand is trying to become. The Mask adapts; the Jester makes you notice.

Jokers · Wild

Starborn Changeling. You can go back for a missed chance, but you can't stay in the past. (House rule blank.)
