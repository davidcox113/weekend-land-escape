A separate Verdant story

Verdant Kingdom · Mahjong Cards

A seed falls where no one is looking, someone tends it, and patient care grows a bridge, gifts, a garden between kingdoms, a storm that bends but does not break, friends who mend it, and at last a great tree that shelters all—and drops a new seed so the story can begin again.

Mahjong cards only. These are American-mahjong-style Sudoku/story tiles (Verdant / Bams), hosted here for viewing only. They are not Weekend Land Escape game cards and are not used in that game.

Mahjong lesson: a Bam run is built one tile at a time; rushing breaks a good hand. Life lesson: small things cared for patiently grow roots that outlast storms.
