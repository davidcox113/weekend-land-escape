The game

Build a kingdom. Then defend it.

Weekend Land Escape is a two-player fantasy kingdom card game with its own gold economy and fortification systems. The gallery holds 159 cards: 64 Wave 1 catalog cards, plus previews of six race waves, 38 Wave 2 cards and 12 stand-alone monsters.

Lands & Mana

Play one land per turn and tap it for mana in one of six custom marks. Hybrid lands and mana rocks fix your colors.

Creatures

Scrags, orc warbands and legendary dragons fight with power and toughness. Keywords like Aggressive, Armored and Siege give each unit its edge.

Artifacts & Equipment

Mana rocks accelerate you; spears, hauberks, banners and runed plate turn soldiers into champions — for a price in gold.

Fortifications

Walls, bunkers, towers and keeps use DEFENSE instead of power/toughness — shielding your troops and your markets.

Gold Economy

Markets, mines and war chests bring gold in; upkeep, equip costs and mythic dragons send it out. Manage the flow.

No Dice Needed

Combat is pure power versus toughness — no random rolls. Track life and gold with Treasure tokens, a scoresheet or a phone.

Mana marks

Six Marks and Gold

Every spell draws on the six mana marks of the realm. Gold, tracked with Treasure tokens, is the second resource.

Bastion

White · order
Defense, discipline, healing and righteous steel.

Harbor

Blue · tempo
Draw, dig, bounce and battlefield timing.

Mire

Black · removal
Attrition, taxes and hard answers.

Forge

Red · aggression
Rush, pressure and warbands.

Wildwood

Green · ramp
Growth, mana acceleration and big threats.

Quarry

Colorless · structures
Artifacts, mana rocks, forts and walls.

Gold

The second resource
Treasure tokens: +1 gold each. Pays for upkeep, Equip and mythics.
